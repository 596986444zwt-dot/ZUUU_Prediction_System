# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为规范

## Our commitment

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone should be able to ask questions, contribute research, and discuss engineering choices without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while checking technical claims against evidence.
- Critique ideas and code, not a contributor's identity or character.
- Help newcomers with clear, practical feedback; accept constructive criticism.
- Credit others' work, respect data/source licenses, and disclose limitations honestly.
- Keep discussions focused and protect confidential information and personal privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language, unwanted attention, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Publishing another person's credentials or private information is prohibited.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to repository Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, reviews, and project spaces where a participant represents the project. For public conduct problems, ask maintainers to moderate without repeating abusive or private material. For sensitive reports, use the private reporting contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not create a public vulnerability report for an ordinary technical disagreement. When the private channel is unavailable, request a private contact without posting details.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request corrections, warn participants, limit participation, or block repeated/severe violations. Responses should be proportionate and protect reporter privacy. A participant may request reconsideration with new context through a private channel. Maintainers are subject to the same standards; this policy does not promise a response time or disclose confidential moderation records.

## 中文摘要

尊重不同背景和经验的参与者，基于证据讨论技术，对事不对人。禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、泄露个人信息或凭证，以及持续干扰讨论。维护者可采取删除内容、警告、限制参与或封禁等适度措施。敏感问题通过安全文档中的私密渠道报告，普通技术分歧在 Issue / PR 中理性讨论。
